Basically Bitcoin is the anti-religion : it exposes the implicit bible of everyone else.

That's pretty much it, you know. There are to this day people resisting email. Among them, famous people, intellectually respectable people, people I personally like a lot.ii What difference does it make ?

Since we are on the topic : there are supposedly still people who resist bathing. I've never met any personally, but then again I live a very sheltered life.iii That does make a difference, of course, but certainly not to soap, or bathroom implements, or washing supplies merchants. The only place where not washing makes a difference is strictly subsumed to the slovenly in question : it severely limits their social, economical and political prospects.

Exactly the same is true of Bitcoin, incidentally. The choice of not getting into the WoT in 2012 - because it is a choice, nobody said you have to make it consciously for it to countiv, you choose by act and you choose by omission equally well - has already cost you. For instance, you can't start a MPEx security today. Maybe next year. So you can't countv in Bitcoin finance this year. Maybe next year.

The choice to stay away, whether voluntary or involuntary, whether deliberate or through omission, whether because you're poor or because you're stupid has already cost you, and will continue to cost you for the conceivable future.

And what is the price ? Why, just your social, economical and political perspectives. With every passing day you are becoming more of a nigger, in the proper sense of that term : enslaved, to the will of others, who not only think they are better than you, but also hold the whips, shackles and irons to make it plain for your needs and benefit. Nothing to do with skin color, everything to do with cryptographical cross section.

With every passing day your chances to be part of tomorrow's elite dwindle, and your chances to be part of tomorrow's downtrodden, hungry masses yearning to breathe free increase.

With every passing day Bitcoin doesn't die, your future dies a little more.
