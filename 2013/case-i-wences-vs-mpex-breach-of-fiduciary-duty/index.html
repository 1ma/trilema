    I can confirm that the round 130 BTC transaction showing in the blockchain has no known destination or purpose. I can also confirm it was close in time to a deposit order for 130 BTC, which was never paid.     I believe that the OP can prove beneficial ownership of the 130 BTC either by showing the MPEx signed DEPOSIT slip for the unpaid order. Alternatively, if they can quote the exact sum I am prepared it to confirm it. I think this would constitute proof because I can't see a way in which a third party would reasonably have obtained that information. Alternatively, signing a declaration with the sending address (19StxPcpGtY4vcqLNoU76hBfKUwhUR7Xst) would constitute reasonable proof also.

    In general mispaid/rounded orders create significant overhead for MPEx, but exceptionally in this one case I am not pursuing compensation. Judges should however bear in mind that the prevailing party receives a fraction of the fees paid, and consequently entering a judgement for 100% in favour of the claimant would result in a 140 BTC award (130 claimed + 10 in fee). A 92.85% award would result in a ~130 BTC disbursement (~120.7 from the claim and ~9.3 from the fee) making the claimant whole. A lower award would in effect impose a fine on him.
