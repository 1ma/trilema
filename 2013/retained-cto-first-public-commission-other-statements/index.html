Retained CTO. First public commission. Other statements.

Friday, 14 June, Year 5 d.Tr. | Author: Mircea Popescu

The good news is that I've managed to retain the help of Marti McCuller for the S.MG project.

The deal is that she puts in no less than 20 hours a week for a year, consisting principally of supervision of the untanglement of any codeballs that may in the course of business need untangling as well as supervision of software design. In the good tradition of software engineering, "supervision" means, of course, "do it yourself if you can't find somebody who can do it". She will have no game design nor art responsibilities (so if the quests or graphics suck you'll have to argue with someone else).

In compensation she's agreed to take 10.85 mn shares issued under a SSW at 1.2 par callable in six months. Consequently, as per 3.1(e) a Special Stock Warrant is hereby issued, as follows :

    The first order of business is to select an amenable game engine, a process which ideally should not extend for more than two weeks.

    To best use this interval, S.MG will organise a drawing contest open to the community.

    The specifications are as follows : 2048x1536 pixels png, medieval setting (this means no spaceships and no space explosions, sorry).

    What you do : The splash screen for our flagship game. This includes the once in a lifetime opportunity to name it.ii

    What you get : Winner gets 1.337 BTCiii because he's so cool, mention by name on the game credits and likely plenty of more work in the not-so-distant futureiv. Other participants may also get further commissions, the entire "winner" thing is more symbolic than anything. Pretty much anyone participating with good stuffv is going to be a winner, because here at S.MG a winner is you.

    By submitting artwork you warrant and represent that you have the right to do so, and you assign the rights thereof to S.MG. Please leave a link in the comment section under this article, we don't review emailed files or privately sent links. Results will be announced Jun 28th. The viewing public is encouraged to offer its own critique, but teh artists may rest easy in the secure knowledge that S.MG management doesn't really care what anyone says.
