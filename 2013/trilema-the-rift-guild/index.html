The 11th most prolific alchemist on the entire shard, at your service. (Data is innacurate as I haven't really been doing this for more than four days or so).

But anyway, back to business : Trilema, it's a guild on Phynnious which is the (only) PVP shard on the EU servers of Rift, which is free to playi and works in Wine. Come say hi!
