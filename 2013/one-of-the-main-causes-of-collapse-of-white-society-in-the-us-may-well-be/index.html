One of the main causes of collapse of white society in the US may well be...

Wednesday, 23 January, Year 5 d.Tr. | Author: Mircea Popescu

Me I got some q's for you.
Her Sup?

Me What you think of teh girl ?
Her Well of what I can see, meh.

Me Meh isn't much.
Her Don't particularly like her shape anywhere, maybe her lower legs...hate the whole "I hold this item in my hands it's meaningful" sorta pose.... unremarkable other than for the fact she's naked.

Me Read the text ?
Her No, lemme read. Ok, she's sincere and sensitive and fairly well-spoken.

Me Aha! She's certainly a slave. Not a bad one for sure, likely a good one. Is it ?
Her Yeah.

Me Is she a prostitute ?
Her I doubt it.

Me Me too. Do you think she's happy ?
Her With the note that I really dont know how to judge something like that, maybe. Leaning towards yes, I'd guess, of a maybe.

Me Would you say being a man's slave is likely to be more fulfilling a fate for a buncha women than the Jewish ideal (what the US calls "romantic") ?
Her Define bunch? For some tens of thousands maybe, iono. For most, certainly not.

Me At least a fifth of the population.
Her No.

Me I think prolly like a third tbh.
Her Heh.

Me The monogamous "romantic" bs is prolly satisfying (marginally) for a woman in 3. Ie, the neurotic ones that don't juice properly.
Her What do you have to do?

Me Hm ?
Her To be in a monogamous "romantic" relationship, what do you have to do?

Me Sex and the city.
Her I'm not sure what that means. You have to pretend you're in a tv show?

Me You're completely unfamiliar with the expectations, aspirations and practices as depicted by that ?
Her I'm not, it's just kinda vague is all.

Me So is slavery, in the end. Human relations are like a smoke.
Her Hm.

Me Would you say slavery is more common among the black than among the white ? I'm not talking of the play-pretend slavery-as-theatrics, which is afaik unknown in black communities, but of the genuine article.
Her I have no idea. I would think it'd be shunned among black communities in the US more, but I really dont know.

Me I suddenly realised, looking at that pic, that in fact it's likely to be about as prevalent as it's desirable, among blacks. Which gives a great advantage.
Her Desirable by whom? The people involved?

Me Yep.
Her Aha... that's an interesting point.

Me One of the main causes of collapse of white society in the US may very well be muchly superior intimate arrangements among the non-whites.
Her Wow, what a concept.

Me Why live the life of yet another Carrie when you could be the OGs naked & chained slut ?
Her Does this only work for black people?

Me Nope.

So there you have it. Frightening, isn't it ? Comments welcome.
