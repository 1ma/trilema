Zhukov Check this out :

Annanas lol

Zhukov Well ?
Annanas Seems fairly straightforward that sure while the pic is "all affirmative", it elicits negative feelings in poster, which must be attributed to person who made pic, otherwise it's an entirely self-contained problem in which case it'd be readily solvable. Does the nonsense make sense?

Zhukov Now it does ty!

Time for a little bit of re-education for all you trolls of the Interwebs. Copy these down in your notebooks one hundred times :

Positive : 1. That is based on an impartial evaluation of immediate observables, without regard for ideology or cognitive bias.
(see Positivism).
WRONG 2. That is certain, or has been verified, or can or should be relied upon.
("I'm positive I had the keys this morning - how else did I lock the door ?")
WRONG 3. That presents a quality, or contains a component, or displays a characteristic of interest.
(such as in "HIV positive")
WRONG 4. That proceeds in a measurable space away from the origin in such a manner that later elements or states are larger than earlier elements or states.
("Positive integer" ; through abuse : "positive evolution")
0. That which fosters and supports my chosen biases.
Affirmative : 1. A statement which does not employ the logical operand not (!, ¬) or its grammatical equivalents.
("This sentence is affirmative. This one is not.").
WRONG 2. (geeky). A verbose replacement for "yes".
("Are you a total dork ?" "Affirmative!")
0. Any public utterance of positive bullcrap.
Ignorant : 1. Unaware of facts previously established.
0. That which does not observe the above defintiions.
Feminism : 1. The notion that court testimony from a woman is as sound as if it came from a man.
WRONG 2. The notion that votes cast by women have the same validity and intrinsic value as votes cast by meni.

WRONG 3. The notion that while women on average work fewer hours during the year than men, their yearly pay should nevertheless be equal ; the notion that while women suck at most things they try and (unlike men) generally display the good sense not to persist it should nevertheless be the case that an equal number of men and women are found in all those fringe places where a good deal of luck is always required to supplement whatever natural talents one might possess.ii

0. Affirming the positive of women for that reason.

PS. For the record : nobody sees a fat chick as "beautiful". How she "sees herself" can strictly decide whether she will be perceived as merely fat, or fat and very annoying. Beauty requires actual work, none of this "seeing yourself" bullcrap.
