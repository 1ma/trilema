Muscle Tracer posted:

This may be naively optimistic of me, but does the bitcoin hashing process perform redundant work? Like, if I hash 1311654ads3fa24sdf354awerf321, is someone else going to hash that again, or does it actually keep a master list of hashed values somewhere?
My understanding is that mining hashes part of the previous block plus new transactions from the network plus some random data in search of a hash less than the current difficulty. Linking each block to the previous is what makes it a block chain and makes each block as difficult to solve as the last one. And nothing keeps a record of the results; a GPU can compute hundreds of millions of hashes each second, which would fill up a 1tb hard drive in a matter of minutes. As far as I know, the hashed result doesn't even get sent back to the CPU unless it's a winner.
